# Rapabi Digital — Internal Client Tracker Guide

## Purpose
Use `client-tracker.html` to keep a simple record of enquiries, quotes, project progress, client approvals, payment status and delivery.

## Open the tracker
After the tracker change is merged and deployed, open:

`/business/client-tracker.html`

The website deployment remains protected by Vercel SSO. This is an internal tool, not a public customer portal.

## How it works
- Records are saved in the browser's local storage on the device/browser profile where you use the tracker.
- The tracker has no database, account system, analytics, or server submission.
- Records do not automatically sync between your phone and another device or browser.
- Use **Export backup** regularly and keep the JSON backup somewhere private.
- Use **Import backup** to restore or combine a previous backup. Matching record IDs are replaced; other records are kept.
- Clearing browser/site data, changing browser profiles, or losing the device may remove the local records if you do not have a backup.

## Suggested workflow
1. Add a record when a genuine enquiry arrives.
2. Record only the details needed to respond and scope the work.
3. Move the stage from New enquiry through Discovery and Quote sent.
4. Record the agreed quote and written client approval before production.
5. Update payment, approval, delivery and target date as the project progresses.
6. Mark Completed only after the agreed deliverables are handed over.

## Privacy and safety
- Do not store passwords, identity numbers, bank/card details, private credentials, or sensitive personal information.
- Do not store client passwords or account access in notes.
- Use a device lock and keep exported backups private.
- This browser-only tracker is not encrypted storage or a regulated client-record system. It is a lightweight operational aid, not legal, tax or accounting software.

## Current limits
- No automatic email or WhatsApp capture.
- No invoices, payment processing, reminders, or customer messaging.
- No cloud sync or multi-user access.
- No customer outreach or publishing actions.
