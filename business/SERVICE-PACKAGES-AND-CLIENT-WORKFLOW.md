# Rapabi Digital — Service Packages & Client Workflow

**Internal working document · South Africa · Free-first operations**

These are proposed introductory offers for planning and discussion. They are not verified market averages, binding quotations, or public price commitments. Confirm the scope and final price in writing before accepting any project.

## 1. Service menu

### A. One-page website — proposed R900–R1,500
**Best for:** a small business that needs a simple online presence.

Included:
- One responsive, single-page website.
- Sections agreed before work starts (for example: hero, services, about, gallery and contact).
- One enquiry route such as WhatsApp or email.
- Basic page title and description.
- Mobile layout check and one handover walkthrough.
- Up to two reasonable revision rounds on the agreed design and supplied content.

Client supplies:
- Business name, contact details, service information and approved wording.
- Logo and photos they own or have permission to use.
- Any required links and preferred colours.

Not included unless separately agreed:
- Domain purchase, paid hosting, paid email, ecommerce, booking systems, copywriting from scratch, ongoing maintenance, paid stock or third-party subscriptions.

### B. Graphic design — proposed R150–R350 per simple design
**Best for:** a single social post, flyer or promotional graphic.

Included:
- One design for one agreed purpose and platform/size.
- Use of client-supplied brand details and approved copy.
- One initial concept and up to two reasonable revision rounds.
- Final export in an agreed common image format.

Not included:
- Printing, paid stock/assets, complex illustration, full brand identity systems, or unlimited variations.

### C. Social media content support — proposed R500–R900 per month
**Best for:** a small business that needs a consistent, manageable content starter plan.

Starter scope:
- Eight static posts per month.
- Basic monthly content outline.
- Captions based on client-approved information.
- One consolidated review round before the monthly batch is finalised.
- Files supplied ready for the client to publish.

Not included by default:
- Daily community management, replying to customers, paid advertising, ad spend, guaranteed reach or sales, photography, video production, or posting through client accounts. Account access and publishing would require a separate written scope and explicit client permission.

### D. Short-form video edit — proposed R150–R350 per simple edit
**Best for:** a short vertical clip using footage supplied by the client.

Included:
- One short edit from supplied, usable footage.
- Basic cuts, simple titles/captions and music only where the client has usage rights.
- One export in an agreed vertical format.
- Up to one reasonable revision round.

Not included:
- Filming, advanced motion graphics, extensive subtitles, paid music/assets, or multiple platform versions unless agreed.

## 2. Scope and quote rules

Before work begins, write down:
1. The exact deliverable and quantity.
2. The format, dimensions or page sections.
3. What the client must supply and by when.
4. The agreed price and any third-party costs.
5. The expected timeline, including when the clock starts (normally after required materials and the agreed initial payment are received).
6. Included revision rounds and what counts as a new request.
7. Delivery method and final approval process.

A request that changes the agreed brief, adds deliverables or requires extra revisions should be paused and re-quoted before extra work begins. Never promise specific sales, followers, views, rankings or business results.

## 4. Delivery workflow

1. **Enquiry:** acknowledge the request; do not promise availability or a price before reviewing the brief.
2. **Discovery:** collect the relevant onboarding details and assets.
3. **Quote:** send a written scope, proposed price, exclusions, timeline and revision limit.
4. **Approval:** obtain the client's written acceptance of the scope before starting.
5. **Initial payment:** if using a deposit, state the amount, due date, refund/cancellation terms and milestones clearly in writing before payment. Do not start paid work until the agreed arrangement is confirmed. Keep records and follow applicable consumer and tax rules; seek qualified advice where needed.
6. **Production:** create the agreed deliverables and keep the client updated at agreed checkpoints.
7. **Review:** send a preview with a clear feedback deadline; collect feedback in one consolidated message where possible.
8. **Revisions:** complete only the included revision rounds. Quote scope changes separately and get approval first.
9. **Final delivery:** provide the agreed final files/links after the agreed payment conditions are met.
10. **Close-out:** confirm receipt, provide a short handover note and ask permission before using the work publicly as a portfolio example.

## 5. Suggested internal operating rules

- Start with one small, clearly scoped project rather than accepting a large open-ended job.
- Keep client approval in writing.
- Do not publish client work, testimonials, logos or results in the Rapabi Digital portfolio without permission.
- Never describe fictional portfolio concepts as completed client projects.
- Do not buy domains, subscriptions, stock assets or advertising on a client's behalf without written approval and a clear budget.
- Keep backups of final deliverables, but do not keep unnecessary personal data.
- Do not guarantee outcomes controlled by platform algorithms or the client's market.
