# Rapabi Digital — Client Enquiry & Quotation Templates

**Internal templates · Do not send without checking and completing every placeholder**

Use these templates to respond consistently. Confirm the actual scope, price, availability and timeline before sending. The prices in the service-package document are proposed starting points only, not automatic quotes.

---

## 1. First WhatsApp reply

Hello! Thank you for contacting Rapabi Digital.

We'd be happy to learn more about what you need. Could you please share:

1. Your business name and what your business does.
2. Which service you're interested in: website design, graphic design, social media content or video editing.
3. What you'd like us to create and what outcome you're hoping for.
4. Your preferred deadline.
5. Any examples, brand materials or content you can share.

Once we understand the project, we'll review the requirements and send you a clear scope and quotation. No work will begin until you approve the details.

Rapabi Digital  
Build. Connect. Deliver.

## 2. Short acknowledgement after receiving a brief

Thank you for sharing those details. We've received your project information and will review the requirements.

We'll come back with the proposed deliverables, price, timeline, what's included and any exclusions. This message is an acknowledgement only, not confirmation that the project has been accepted or scheduled.

To accept this quotation, reply in writing with:

No work is booked or started until Rapabi Digital confirms acceptance and the agreed starting conditions are met.

## Internal send-before-check list

- [ ] Correct client and business name.
- [ ] Correct email/WhatsApp and project details.
- [ ] Exact deliverables, quantities and file formats.
- [ ] Price and third-party costs clearly stated.
- [ ] Timeline is realistic and includes client feedback time.
- [ ] Revision allowance and exclusions are clear.
- [ ] Payment, cancellation and refund terms are written clearly.
- [ ] Client has approved the quote in writing.
- [ ] No passwords or unnecessary personal data are requested.
- [ ] No unapproved spending, outreach, publishing or portfolio use.

**Important:** These are operational templates, not a legal contract or tax advice. Before taking on paid work, make sure your business, invoicing, consumer-facing terms and tax obligations are handled appropriately for your circumstances.
