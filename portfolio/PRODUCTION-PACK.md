# Rapabi Digital — Portfolio Production Pack

Status: preparation only. These are fictional concept projects, not client work. Do not describe them as commissioned work or publish them as real customer results.

## Portfolio direction

Brand: Rapabi Digital  
Promise: Build. Connect. Deliver.  
Visual system: premium, clear, mobile-first; purple and near-black for Rapabi Digital's own identity. Client concepts should have their own visual identities rather than all looking like Rapabi Digital.

## Project 01 — Northstar Coffee
Type: fictional café concept / graphic design and social content

### Creative direction
Warm, welcoming, modern neighbourhood café. Palette: espresso brown, oat cream and a restrained copper accent. Use editorial typography, generous whitespace and close-up coffee imagery.

### Sample Instagram post copy
Headline: YOUR WEEKEND, BREWED BETTER  
Supporting line: Slow mornings. Fresh coffee. Good company.  
Call to action: VISIT US THIS WEEKEND  
Disclosure label: CONCEPT DESIGN  
Footer: A concept by Rapabi Digital

### Deliverables to create
- One square Instagram promotional post.
- One matching story adaptation.
- One caption with a clear, non-fabricated call to action.

Caption:
"Make a little room for a slower morning. Northstar Coffee is a fictional café concept created to demonstrate how a consistent visual direction can bring a social post to life. Concept design by Rapabi Digital."

## Project 02 — Glow Studio
Type: fictional beauty studio / social media content system

### Creative direction
Calm, contemporary beauty studio. Palette: soft lilac, warm ivory, charcoal and muted rose. Keep layouts clean, accessible and readable on a phone. Avoid fake testimonials, before-and-after claims, medical claims, prices or appointment details.

### Sample post copy
Headline: YOUR MOMENT. YOUR GLOW.  
Supporting line: A little time set aside for you.  
Call to action: ENQUIRE ABOUT SERVICES  
Disclosure label: FICTIONAL BRAND CONCEPT  
Footer: Social content concept by Rapabi Digital

### Deliverables to create
- One service-introduction post.
- One educational/self-care reminder post.
- One story template.
- A 1-week sample content calendar.

### Sample content calendar
1. Monday — Brand introduction: what the studio concept represents.
2. Wednesday — General self-care reminder, with no health promises.
3. Friday — Service-category spotlight, clearly illustrative and without invented prices.

## Project 03 — Apex Digital
Type: fictional local professional service / one-page website concept

### Creative direction
Confident, modern and practical. Palette: deep navy, white, electric violet accent. Prioritise clear service descriptions, trust-building information and simple contact paths.

### Sample landing-page copy
Eyebrow: DIGITAL SUPPORT FOR GROWING BUSINESSES  
Headline: Make your next move with confidence.  
Supporting copy: A fictional business concept showing how a small service provider can explain what it does, who it helps and how to enquire.  
Primary CTA: MAKE AN ENQUIRY  
Sections: Services / How it works / About / Contact  
Disclosure: FICTIONAL WEBSITE CONCEPT — NOT A REAL BUSINESS.

### Deliverables to create
- Mobile-first one-page website.
- WhatsApp and email enquiry links using clearly marked placeholders in the concept.
- Basic desktop and mobile screenshots.
